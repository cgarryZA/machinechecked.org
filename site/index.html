MachineChecked puts a mathematical paper beside its Lean formalisation. Select a statement to explore its Lean counterpart, see what has been proved, and find what still needs review.

Open a paper

Browse the catalogue or open a paper in your Lean project. Select a numbered statement to begin.

02

Compare the statements

Read the paper, every formal hypothesis and the exact Lean together. Open the definitions that give the statement its meaning.

03

Read, review, share

Compare the statements, record a review, and share the HTML reader.

Use it on your paper

In your Lean project's directory, build the modules you want to inspect and run:

lake build
uvx machinechecked view

Runs locally in your Lean project. Use uvx machinechecked build to export an HTML reader.

Requires Python 3.11+, Lean, and TeX Live for LaTeX or Poppler for PDFs.
